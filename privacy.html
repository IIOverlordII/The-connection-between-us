Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

In short: there are no accounts with us, no cookies of our own and no analytics. Your test answers stay on your device (and, if you turn it on, in your own Google Drive), and we never receive them. If you buy the full version, the payment is handled by Paddle, and we only learn what is needed to unlock it.

Who is responsible

Between Us is made by Aureliel, who is responsible for your data under this policy. Contact: support@aureliel.store.

Your answers and results

Test answers, names of participants, game answers and settings are stored only on your device: in your browser's storage for the website, or in the folder %APPDATA%\Psy-Profile for the Windows program. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, unless you turn on saving to Google Drive (see below). You can delete them at any time in Settings → “Delete all”, or by clearing the site data in your browser.

When you share a participant code, a result or a game invitation, the answers are packed into the link itself, after the # sign. Browsers don't send that part of a link to any server, so it reaches only the people you send it to. Anyone who has such a link can see what it contains, so share it only with people you trust.

Saving to Google Drive (optional)

On the website you can choose “Sign in with Google” in Settings. The App then keeps a copy of your participants, answers, games and the unlock code of a purchase in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, and reads it back on your other devices. The App talks to Google directly from your browser: this data doesn't pass through our servers, and we can't see it.

The App asks Google for two permissions only: access to its own hidden folder in your Drive (it can't see or change your other files), and your email address, which is shown in Settings so you know which account is connected and stays on your device. Google's sign-in script is loaded only when you sign in. Google processes this data under Google's Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: it is used only to save and restore your data in the App, it is never sold, shared or used for advertising, and nobody reads it.

You can sign out in Settings at any time; your data then stays on your device and in your Drive. To delete the copy in Drive, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Between Us → Delete hidden app data. To withdraw the App's access, visit your Google Account's third-party connections.

Visiting the website

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data needed to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks: your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. An older copy of the site on GitHub Pages, hosted by GitHub, only forwards visitors to aureliel.store. We don't set cookies and don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The App stores its own files on your device so that it works offline.

Buying the full version

Payments are handled by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle collects the data needed for the payment and for taxes, such as your name, email address, country and payment details, and processes it under Paddle's Privacy Policy. We never see your card details. Paddle shares with us the order details: your email address, country, order number and what you bought.

To unlock the full version, the App sends the order number, or the email address you enter for “Restore purchase”, to our server at aureliel.store. The server checks the purchase with Paddle and sends back an unlock code that is stored on your device. About once a week, while you are online, the App sends the order number again to check that the purchase was not refunded. The server does not keep a database of buyers.

The checkout window is provided by Paddle. Its script loads from Paddle only when you open the checkout, and Paddle may use cookies inside it as described in its policy.

Emails

If you write to us, we use your message and email address only to answer you and to handle your purchase or refund. Mail to support@aureliel.store is forwarded by Cloudflare to our mailbox.

Why we use this data

We process purchase data to fulfil our contract with you (to unlock what you bought and to handle refunds) and to meet tax and accounting obligations. Technical data is processed for our legitimate interest in keeping the website secure and working. We don't sell personal data and don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept by Paddle as long as tax and accounting laws require. We keep support emails as long as needed to answer them and to handle any related purchase questions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use and to complain to a data protection authority. Write to support@aureliel.store. Your answers on your device are fully under your control and can be deleted in the App.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 16.

Data transfers

Cloudflare, Paddle and, if you use saving to Google Drive, Google may process data in countries other than yours. They use legal safeguards for such transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
