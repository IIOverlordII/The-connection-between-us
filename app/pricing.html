Between Us — personality and compatibility test

Between Us is a web app (it also installs on phones and computers, and comes as a Windows program). Each person takes a personality test and gets a detailed profile. Then the app compares people: couples, friends, families or teams. It also includes a game for two, “Who knows the partner better”. The app works in 10 languages and offline, and the answers stay on the user's own device.
