Refund Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee. If the full version of Between Us isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase and you will get your money back in full. You don't need to give a reason.

How to ask for a refund

  • Use the link in the receipt email from Paddle, or find your order at paddle.net, or
  • write to support@aureliel.store from the email address you used at checkout, or include your order number.

What happens next

Our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders, sends the money back to the payment method you used. Most banks show it within 5–10 business days. The full version is switched off once the refund is processed. The free features keep working as before.

After 14 days

If the full version doesn't work for you because of a problem on our side and we can't fix it, write to us: we will refund you even after 14 days. Your rights under the consumer protection laws of your country are not affected.
